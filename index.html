Privacy Policy

Auto Clicker for Android · Version 1.0 · Effective 28 August 2026

com.epicproject.automatictap.autoclicker
Auto Clicker collects nothing.

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and the app does not hold the INTERNET permission — so it is technically incapable of sending your data anywhere.

  • No data leaves your phone
  • No internet permission
  • No ads, no trackers, no account
  • Screen content is never read

1. What Auto Clicker stores

Everything the app remembers is written to a single private settings file inside its own sandbox on your device:

  • The screen coordinates of the points you place, and the swipe start and end points.
  • Timing values: interval, randomness, hold time, repeat count, start delay and time limit.
  • Presets you save, along with the name you give them.
  • Appearance and behaviour preferences such as accent colour, AMOLED mode and haptics.
  • Simple counters: total taps dispatched, number of sessions, longest session length.

No other app can read that file, and Auto Clicker never copies it anywhere.

2. What Auto Clicker never does

  • It does not read, record, screenshot or transmit the contents of your screen.
  • It does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, advertising ID, device identifiers or usage analytics.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs.
  • It does not create an account or sync anything to a server. There is no server.

3. Why the Accessibility permission is required

Android only allows an app to dispatch a synthetic tap through an Accessibility Service. That single capability is the entire reason Auto Clicker asks for it.

The service is declared with canRetrieveWindowContent="false". That means the operating system does not hand Auto Clicker the text, layout or contents of any window — not even its own. It can dispatch gestures, and nothing else.

The service does no work at all until you press Start. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings › Accessibility, and the app will stop immediately.

4. The full permission list

  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE — dispatches the taps and swipes you configure.
  • VIBRATE — the short haptic tick when you press a control. Optional; turn it off under More › Behaviour.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — optional. Granting it lets the floating panel reopen the app while another app is in front. The panel itself works without it.

Auto Clicker requests no other permission. In particular it does not request INTERNET, so no network connection of any kind is possible.

5. Sharing and selling

There is nothing to share and nothing to sell. Auto Clicker discloses no information to third parties, affiliates, advertisers, data brokers or anyone else, because it never collects any in the first place.

6. Backups

If you have Android backup switched on for your device, the operating system may include Auto Clicker's settings file in the encrypted backup of your own Google account, exactly as it does for other apps. That copy belongs to you and is governed by Google's terms, not by Auto Clicker.

To exclude it, turn off backup for this app in Android Settings › Google › Backup.

7. Your controls

  • Erase all data: More › Privacy and data › Erase all app data. This clears every point, preset, counter and preference immediately.
  • Reset counters only: More › Statistics › Reset.
  • Revoke gesture access: Android Settings › Accessibility › Auto Clicker › off.
  • Uninstall: removes the settings file with it. Nothing is left behind.

8. Children

Auto Clicker is a general-purpose utility. It is not directed at children and it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.
